Tally Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Tally | Time Calculator ("Tally") adds and subtracts lengths of time. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Tally does not collect your information.

What Tally stores

Everything you create in Tally, such as saved marks, folders, and your settings (style, fonts, haptics, time limit), is stored only on your device. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

What Tally does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name, email, or any personal details.
  • It does not collect analytics, usage data, or crash reports.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not track you or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Backups you choose to make

Tally can export your saved marks as text so you can keep a backup. When you do, iOS shows its standard share sheet, and you decide where the text goes (for example Notes or Files). Tally does not send it anywhere on its own.

Apple

If you install Tally through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy. That is outside Tally's control.

Children

Tally collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
