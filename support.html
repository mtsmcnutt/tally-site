Common questions

How do I add and subtract times?

Spin the wheels to set minutes and seconds, then tap + to add it or − to subtract it. The wheels reset to zero each time. Everything you enter shows in the line above the total. Tap = to finish the line.

How do I save a total?

Tap the save button on the time card to keep the total as a mark. The folder button opens your marks, where you can load one into the total, or copy, move, rename, or delete it. Tap the ... button on a mark or folder for those actions.

What is Clock mode?

Tap the Clock button at the top to start from a time of day, like 9:17 AM, and add or subtract from it. Tap the M / S button to go back to plain lengths of time.

How do I back up my marks?

Open Settings, go to the Advanced tab, and choose Export marks. Keep the text somewhere safe, like Notes or Files. To restore it, choose Import marks and paste it back in.

How do I change how the app looks?

Open Settings and go to the Style tab. You can choose a theme, light or dark, a color theme, and the font for the numbers.

Can I see the tutorial again?

Yes. Open Settings, go to the Help tab, and choose Replay the tutorial.
